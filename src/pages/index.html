Fix log

Things that did not work, what was actually wrong, and what I changed. Hardware first.

The drone landed in the funnel but would not slide to center

Fixed on hardware

Drone dock

Cause
The surface, not the landing gear. On a 16° wall a foot slides only below a friction coefficient of 0.29, and raw MDF with proud screw heads is above it.
Fix
Screws sunk flush, flaps faced with UHMW tape. It slides now, and a $200 to $300 landing-gear redesign was never built.

The boat's battery port answered nothing

Fixed on hardware

Boat control and telemetry

Cause
I had assumed logic-level serial. A 160 ohm reading across the data pair showed it was RS485, and the protocol was vendor-specific.
Fix
A probe that sweeps eight frame types across six baud rates found it at 19,200. Decoded values checked against the live pack.

Laser-cut joints that have to press together by hand

Fixed on hardware

Drone dock

Cause
Kerf widens every slot, and the first files drew slots at 0.232 in for 0.236 in stock.
Fix
A coupon with six slot widths and three dovetail offsets. The winner, 0.220 in, went back into the script that generates every part.

The control link died on every boot

Worked around on hardware

Boat control and telemetry

Cause
ModemManager grabs the flight controller's serial port at boot, so the router finds it busy and quits.
Fix
A retry loop until the port frees. Honest status: a workaround. The proper service units are staged, not installed.

Charging pad wires kept breaking at the solder joint

Fixed on hardware

Drone dock

Cause
The joint was taking the flex.
Fix
Through-hole connections, epoxy potting and strain relief.

A servo controller board died

Diagnosed on hardware

Boat control and telemetry

Cause
Back-EMF from the servos damaged the main processor of an Arduino UNO R4 WiFi.
Fix
Recovered the board's second processor, an ESP32-S3.
Three more: two caught on paper, one in simulation

A magnetic clamp that would release without anyone noticing

Caught on paper

Drone dock

Cause
Pull and reaction were 60.6 mm apart: a 36.4 N·m couple against about 4.5 N·m from the drone's weight. It would pass any bench test with a hand on the drone.
Fix
Reaction pads moved outboard of the magnet. Found in design review, before anything was built.

A drive belt that could go slack during a landing

Caught on paper

Heave simulator

Cause
The first model shared load evenly between belt spans. Near the end of travel one span is short, far stiffer, and takes nearly all of it.
Fix
Pretension raised from 150 to 250 N. Lowest tension in any case went from minus 18 N to 82 N.

Camera feedback that was no better than no feedback

Fixed in simulation

ShadingBot

Cause
In the first runs the loop watched only the center line of each row while the score averages the full row width: 0.081 against open loop's 0.079.
Fix
Full-width window and a touchdown bug fix: 0.051 against 0.064 on the same early test. Both went in together, so an ablation is still owed. As calibrated, the two methods now tie.
